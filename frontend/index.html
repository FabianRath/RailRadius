About the data

RailRadius only shows rail connections in the countries listed below, built from the operators' public GTFS timetables. Trains outside these countries, or missing from the data, are not shown.

Timetables change and the data can be incomplete or out of date, so I can't guarantee that a displayed connection exists or runs as shown. Please check with the rail operator before you travel.
