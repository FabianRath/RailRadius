## Bugs (data loading)
- [ ] **B29** (High priority) The cross-border name merge (`StationDeduplicator`, Jaccard ≥ 0.4 within 500 m) leaves about 28 stations duplicated within 400 m, found in the full cache of 2026-10-05. Trains don't connect between the two copies. Causes and examples: (1) Cyrillic vs Latin names: 9 stations of the Bulgarian and the Turkish feed, e.g. "Централна гара София" / "SOFYA(SOFIA)", Plovdiv, Ruse; (2) Italian abbreviations: "Bologna Centrale" (Italo) / "BOLOGNA C.LE" (Trenitalia) 3 m apart, "S.DONA' DI PIAVE", "NA Afragola PES", "Szkl. Poręba Górna"; (3) ø not folded: "København H" / "Koebenhavn H" (70 m); (4) one name is part of the other: "Split" / "Split željeznički kolodvor", "Malahide" / "Malahide railway station", Peschiera, Chambéry, Scalea, Fortezza; also Roissy CDG 2 (Eurostar vs Swiss feed) and the sleeper's "Berlin Ostbf" next to "S Ostbahnhof (Berlin)". Possible fixes, in order of reach: merge within the radius when one name's words are all in the other's, ignoring generic words (railway station, Bahnhof, kolodvor, …); fold ø/æ/ß and transliterate Cyrillic; expand common Italian abbreviations (C.LE, S.). Don't merge nearby but separate stations: Madrid Atocha Cercanías / Atocha (124 m) and Málaga M.Z. Cercanías / María Zambrano are separate stations; Avignon Centre / Avignon-TGV show 33 m apart only because one feed has wrong coordinates.
- [ ] **B30** (Low priority) About 18 duplicated stations are 400 m – 2 km apart, mostly because one feed's coordinates are off, so no distance-based rule merges them. Examples: the Swiss feed's French stations Saint-Nabord, Irigny, Saint-Amand-Montrond, La Levade (~1.5 km off), Albertville (Eurostar), Viareggio and Desenzano (Trenord vs Trenitalia), Abfaltersbach and Weitlanbrunn (Austria vs Italy), Hergenrath, Apach, Lauterbourg, Tende, Travedona. About 11 of them carry the same UIC station code on both sides in different forms: `switzerland:8714621` / `france:…OCE87146217` (French form adds a check digit), `italy-trenord:S02084` / `italy:…830002084`, `austria:Pat:47:3877` / `italy:…810003877`. Possible fix: a merge rule matching by UIC code, with one extraction rule per feed ID format. A station can have several codes (København H `8600626` in the Danish feed, `8601309` in the German one), so only equal codes merge and the other rules stay.
- [ ] **B27** (Low priority, hardening; not broken now) The `european-sleeper` feed names its Aachen stop "Aachen Hauptbahnhof" (stop `8015345`), the German feed "Aachen, Hbf". The name merge in `StationDeduplicator` (Jaccard word similarity ≥ 0.4) scores the pair 1/3. Checked in the full cache of 2026-10-05: the stop is merged anyway, by `SharedTrainMatcher`, because the German feed also has the European Sleeper trains (e.g. trains 401 and 453). ES 400 stops at `germany:de:05334:1007:1:1`. The merge depends on that: if a German feed drops the ES trains (or a load runs without the German feed, as in a 2026-10-05 test where it stayed apart from Belgium's "Aachen Hbf (DE)"), Aachen splits in two and the sleeper train no longer connects there. The same can happen to other "Hbf"/"Hauptbahnhof" pairs. Possible fixes: treat "Hbf" and "Hauptbahnhof" as one word when merging, or match stations by UIC code (European Sleeper and Eurostar both use them).
- [ ] **B26** The Belgian feed (dated 2026-02-11) has Nightjet NJ 479 Brussels → Wien on only 10 and 12 Dec 2025: three trips (`…:3413:20251210`, `…:3413:20251210:0`, `…:3413:20251212`) whose services have no weekdays in `calendar.txt` and 1–2 added dates in `calendar_dates.txt`. The real train runs several times a week, so the popup shows "Irregular: 1 day in the timetable" (e.g. Aachen Hbf → Wien Meidling). Check whether a newer feed or the ÖBB feed has the full schedule.
- [ ] **B24** Several GTFS feeds have expired, so their trains can no longer be found on the operators' sites. Last service date per feed (checked 2026-10-05, from `calendar.txt`/`calendar_dates.txt`): greece 2019-01-31, france 2025-04-22, hungary 2025-11-23, bosnia 2025-12-13, croatia 2025-12-14, luxembourg 2026-04-30, denmark 2026-05-06, germany 2026-06-13, poland 2026-06-13. Download current feeds with the update center (admin panel → Feeds, or `scripts/api.sh feeds-download-outdated`), then reload. `s3.transitpdf.com` (greece, hungary, croatia and six others in `gtfs-feeds.txt`) did not answer on 2026-10-05. `gtfs-sources.md` lists replacements on jbb.ghsq.de.
